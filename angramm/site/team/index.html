Team

ANGRAMM was born in Marseille. We imagine, shoot and finish films for brands, clubs and artists, from street documentary to full CGI.

We work as one crew, from the first idea to the final grade, with the same obsession: images that feel true to the places and the people they come from.

Values

  1. Territory

    Every project starts from a place and the people who live there: Marseille, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Nanterre.

  2. Craft

    Creative and technical expertise under one roof, from concept and direction to CGI and post-production.

  3. Partnership

    Major collaborations with high-profile partners, built on trust and on work that lasts.
